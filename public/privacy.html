iWorship — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

iWorship is a hymnal, chord book, and worship-session app built for our congregation. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's kept private. iWorship does not sell your data, run ads, or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

What we collect

iWorship works fully without an account — browsing the hymnal never requires signing in. Signing in (with Google) unlocks favorites, worship-team editing, and hosting a session, and stores a small amount of information tied to your account:

  • Account info — your name, email address, and a unique account ID, provided by Google Sign-In when you choose to sign in.
  • Profile preferences — your display name, your congregation's name (if you enter one), your favorited songs, and display settings like text size and light/dark mode.
  • Worship session activity — if you host or join a live session, the session's name, host, and current song/section are stored while the session is active, along with any chat messages you choose to send in that session (visible to other participants in that same session).
What we don't collect: iWorship does not collect your location, payment or financial information, contacts, photos, health data, or any information beyond what's listed above. There are no ads and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in this app.

How it's stored

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), the same infrastructure used to run the app. It is not shared with any other third party. Access is restricted by security rules — for example, only you can read or write your own profile, and only the worship team can add or edit hymnal content.

Your choices

You can use iWorship without ever signing in. If you've signed in, you can sign out at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account data (see Contact, below) — we'll remove your profile and favorites from our records.

Children's privacy

iWorship is a general-audience congregational app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
